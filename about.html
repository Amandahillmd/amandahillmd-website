About

About Dr. Amanda Hill

I'm a board certified dermatologist and longevity medicine physician, Stanford trained and a Fellow of the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine. Before founding RENEW: Longevity and Regenerative Aesthetics in Shelby Twp, MI, I spent nearly a decade as a Flight Surgeon and dermatologist in the U.S. Air Force.

Here's what years of clinical practice taught me: the way your skin ages is not a surface problem. It's a reflection of your internal biology. Hormones, metabolic health, inflammation, body composition. These drive the visible changes we so often try to treat from the outside in. When a patient comes in for filler, what she frequently needs first is a hormone conversation.

As a physician, wife, and a mom of four, I'm building this work in real time, and I share most of it openly. My aim is to change how men and women think about skin, aging, and health from the inside out.

Beyond my practice, I speak at national medical and industry conferences, advise companies shaping the future of regenerative aesthetics and longevity, and educate fellow physicians on the science of skin longevity. This work lets me push the conversation forward at the level where standards of care are set.

My Philosophy

“Skin ages from the inside out. I treat it that way.”

My approach connects dermatology, regenerative aesthetics, and longevity medicine to support healthier skin and healthier aging. I look beyond what's visible, consider the biology behind it, and prioritize evidence over hype.

01  Skin ages from the inside out

Skin health doesn't exist in isolation. Hormones, metabolic health, nutrition, sleep, inflammation, and muscle all influence how skin changes over time.

02  Look beyond the visible signs of aging

Rather than focusing only on wrinkles, volume loss, or pigmentation, I want to understand the underlying biology and contributing factors.

03  Optimize the patient, then regenerate the tissue

Before any treatment or product, I consider overall health and the biological factors that influence tissue repair and regeneration.

04  Science first. Marketing second

Recommendations are grounded in evidence, with a clear line between what's established, what's promising, and what still needs research. I don't chase every trend or make unsupported promises.

05  The goal isn't to stop aging

The goal is healthy skin function, resilience, and overall health for as long as possible, not a wrinkle-free face or trying to look 25 forever.

06  Treat the whole person, not just the face

A good consultation provides clarity, considers the individual patient, and sometimes means explaining why a requested procedure isn't the best choice.

“Anti-aging is a marketing word. Skin longevity is a medical goal.”

“A good consult should sometimes talk you out of the procedure you came in for.”

“You should leave an appointment with more clarity than pressure.”

If that resonates, you're in the right place.
